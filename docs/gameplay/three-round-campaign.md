## Session and screens
Each round lasts exactly 60,000 ms of the existing game simulation. A new campaign starts with round 1 intro. Each intro names the round, shows its objective and controls, and has a Start Round / Enter action. A 900 ms ready beat follows this deliberate action. Round 1/2 results show that round's scores and a Next Round / Enter action; that action opens the next intro. Round 3 ends at the final summary, with per-round breakdown, P1/P2 totals, team total, best combo and Play Again / R / Enter. Replay starts a new round-1 intro and clears all previous totals. No automatic transition may skip the result or intro. Every timed round proceeds regardless of score; there is no early elimination or undisclosed pass threshold.

Snapshot each round once, before clearing its stats. Completed-round snapshots must not share mutable PlayerStats references. Aggregate each completed round exactly once. Solo excludes P2 from totals and hides irrelevant P2 scorecards. Co-op sums two independent player scorecards. Final best combo is the maximum within a completed round, not a sum across rounds.

Keep the selected input mode and live opted-in camera session across intros and round results. Changing round does not re-request permission or reload the model. Camera Stop continues to select keyboard and stop streams/workers. Stop is usable from final results. Recalibration remains explicit through the existing camera controls. This feature must not weaken existing camera validation to force a start. At each new round, suppress catch/activate until each active player has released the action once; held actions from a previous round must not earn immediate points. Scores never change in intro/results/complete. At round boundaries and after circuit connections, release must be a positive observation from the current input step. A keyboard key-up is valid; camera input must be ready and supply fresh, accepted per-player gesture evidence with the required landmarks visible. Neutral values caused by waiting, lost/stale tracking, obscured gesture landmarks, ambiguity or calibration must not unlock activation. One missing/lost Co-op player must not block the other player when that player remains continuously accepted. Historical catch-up input cannot count as a release. A keyboard-only return-to-menu action may be added to final results if it stops camera explicitly and resets session.

## Shared motion and timing
The scene retains current movement/lane limits. For circuit/valves, divide each active player's own movement range into three equal zones (0 left, 1 middle, 2 right); a target is satisfied by standing in its zone. Keyboard A/D and arrows or torso lean move the robot; S/Down or raise both arms is the same held activation. Existing H0/P2 remain alternative adapters, without adding gestures. All prompt copy must adapt to input mode.

Challenge engine is Phaser/DOM independent and consumes fixed steps no larger than 50 ms; invalid/negative delta consumes zero and oversized single calls clamp to 50 ms. Scene simulation handles full elapsed time but must supply neutral challenge input to historical catch-up steps, using current input only in isCurrentInputStep. This avoids crediting an unobserved hold across a stalled frame. Camera freshness/association safety stays in the existing adapters. No camera frames/landmarks are saved in evidence.

## Round 1 — Energy Catch
Keep the current energy-catch-mvp.md rules and original deterministic core: 60 seconds; cyan +100 with every fifth consecutive catch raising multiplier by 0.5 to a maximum of 3; red hazard -50 with score floor zero and combo reset; miss has no penalty. Solo uses the current solo arena; Co-op keeps two lanes. No tuning of spawn speed, catch window or score rules in this task.

## Round 2 — Duo Circuit
Each active player has a circuit board with three sockets corresponding to the three movement zones. Only the current target is lit. Target order for the first circuit is left, right, middle ([0,2,1]); rotate the order by one index after every completed circuit to vary the route deterministically.

Stand in the target zone and hold activation continuously for 1,000 ms to connect one socket: +100 points, combo +1, bestCombo=max(previous,combo). Wrong zone or released activation clears partial hold only; neither removes earned points nor resets combo. After each successful socket, require a release before accumulating the next hold. Completing three sockets adds a +200 circuit bonus (no extra combo increment), clears the board and starts the next circuit. An action held continuously cannot connect multiple sockets. Show current socket, 0–100% hold progress, connected count 0–3 and completed circuits. Both Solo and Co-op use the same per-player rules.

## Round 3 — Cool the Core
Each active player has a personal core meter starting at 70 (range 0–100) and three valve positions. In each 6,000 ms cycle the active valve follows [middle,left,right] ([1,0,2]) by cycle index. It is safe/cyan for the first 4,000 ms and overloaded/coral for the final 2,000 ms. Show the safe/overload state in words and shape in addition to color, and show which valve is active.

Heat rises 4 units/second continuously. During a safe period, standing at the active valve and holding activation removes 18 units/second in addition to that rise (net -14/s). Every 1,000 continuous ms of safe, correctly positioned activation gives +100 points and combo +1, updating bestCombo. Continuous safe holding can earn another pulse each second. Releasing, leaving the active valve or changing safe/overload phase clears partial pulse progress. Heat clamps to 0–100. These are a game meter and arcade actions, not a physical reactor simulation.

During overload, activating at the active valve adds 16 heat units/second and applies a -50 score penalty once per player per cycle (floor zero), resetting combo. There is no repeated per-frame penalty and no additional hidden heat-100 penalty. Lower arms/release to wait out overload. The round still ends at the timer; remaining heat is shown as feedback and does not silently rescale the score. No gestures faster than the measured tracking cadence are required.
